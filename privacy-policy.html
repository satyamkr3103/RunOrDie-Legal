Run or Die — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Run or Die handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Run or Die may use Unity Player Accounts for account and authentication functionality. Information processed through Unity Player Accounts may include account identifiers and authentication-related information provided by Unity's services.

The game does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings unless a future feature specifically requires it and you are informed appropriately.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide, maintain, secure, and improve account-related functionality, including signing in and associating your game account with game services.

3. Third-Party Services

The game may use services provided by Unity Technologies, including Unity Player Accounts and related Unity services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the game and its connected services. However, no internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Run or Die is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant services or meet legal and security requirements. For questions or requests concerning account information, contact us using the address below.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
